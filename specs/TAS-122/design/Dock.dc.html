Phones only (under 640 px), and only with two or more tabs. 62 px tall, a pill in Dock with the lift and a 2 px transparent border; tabs 46 px tall, 4 px apart: the current one a peach pill with its icon (20 px) and name in Label, the others 64 px wide with their icon (22 px) in On dock.

Props: tabs, two to four of { to, label, icon }, in the order they show. The current tab is the one whose to the page is under (a recipe’s page is under Recipes), from the router, so no page passes it in. Each tab is a link; the current one has aria-current="page" and the others take their label as their accessible name.

Reusable with TopBarTabs: both take the same tabs, so the app keeps one list and each width shows its own. Neither knows about any area (Dino imports none); the app’s shell passes the list.

May grow into: a count on a tab (a dot or number for the shopping list) when a project needs one; not now. Motion: nothing slides between tabs; colours change in 120 ms.
