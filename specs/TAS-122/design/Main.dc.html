Good: one row at every width, so the bar’s height never changes, and phones and tablets read the same: a peach pill with a name, the other tabs as the dock’s icons. The search field keeps its 360 px on a laptop.

Less good: on a tablet only the current tab is named, so the others rest on their icons and accessible names, as the dock’s do. The names come back at 1024 px, where three named tabs, the 360 px search field and the avatar fit side by side.

The tabs are the nav’s links in a list, after the wordmark, so focus meets them before search and the account end at every width. The current one carries aria-current="page". Tabs shown are Picnic’s three; the component takes two to four.
