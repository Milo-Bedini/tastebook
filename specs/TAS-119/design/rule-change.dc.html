Proposed rule change

The boards keep to the rules as they stand; these lines make the departure the spec decided on (ask before publishing) a rule, and record two things the rows don’t yet say. Additions underlined.

Components › Dialog

Now

For anything that can’t be undone: says exactly what happens; the danger button names the action (“Delete account”), the other is “Cancel”, or names what it keeps when the action itself is a cancel (“Keep link”).

Proposed

For anything that can’t be undone: … (“Keep link”). A change that can be undone but reaches beyond the household (making a recipe public) may ask first, with a primary button that names it (“Make public”). Undoing it acts at once, without asking.

Why: principle 3 (NN/g 3) says offer Undo rather than asking. Publishing shows the recipe and Sam’s name to everyone at once, and takes editing from the household: an Undo toast would come after the name was already out. Making it private only narrows who sees it, so it keeps principle 3.

Components › Private mark

Now

… under its title on its page; a public recipe has none.

Proposed

… under its title on its page; a public recipe has none. A household’s public recipe shows “by <name>” in its place, in the text around it (Muted Body on the page, the card’s Small meta), as plain text, never a mark.

Components › Switch

Now

… Disabled keeps its thumb’s end and check, in Field, on a Surface track with a dashed border, under a Muted label and over its reason.

Proposed

… over its reason. While the change it makes is saving, it’s disabled with the reason “Saving…”, and moves only once the change is saved.
