The sheet’s one action is “Make list” (primary); Close, Escape, the scrim and a drag leave without making anything. There’s no Cancel button: the sheet’s close is the way out, as on the ingredients’ sheets.

Who’s it for? is Dino’s SegmentedControl, “Just you” or “Household”, with a help line under it that changes with the choice and says who sees the list: “Only you see it.” or “Everyone in The Bedinis sees it and can change it.” The household is named in the line, not the segment, so a long household name never stretches the track.

Errors say how to fix it: an empty name gets “Give the list a name, like Weeknight dinners.” Names are unique per owner: your own lists among themselves (“You already have a list called Christmas.”), a household’s among the household’s (the line drawn here), so one of each can share a name. “Favourites” is taken in both: “Favourites is already yours. Try another name.” Focus goes to Name; the choice is kept.

Making it closes the sheet and opens the new list’s page, with focus on its heading and a toast “Made <name>” (no Undo: deleting is one press away). While it saves, Make list reads “Making…” with a spinner.
