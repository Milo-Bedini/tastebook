The only way out is the heart: pressing it unsaves (“Removed from Favourites · Undo”) and the card goes. Nothing else to do, so nothing else to press.

Who sees it: anyone outside the author’s household who saved it. The author’s household still sees the recipe itself, private. If it goes public again, the card turns back into the recipe’s polaroid.

Kept where it was saved, not moved to the end, so the grid doesn’t reshuffle under someone looking for it.

No name of who did it: “Made private or deleted” covers both without saying more about another household than the list already did (UK GDPR, minimisation).

Opening its old address shows “Recipe not found”, as today; this card never links there.
