What changes in RecipePhoto: it takes a photo Tastebook stores (the same thumbnails list, made on upload at the widths the page asks for) as well as one from Wikimedia Commons. Its sizes, shapes, lazy loading and the filler when it can’t load stay as they are.

No credit for an uploaded photo. The page passes a caption only for a Commons photo, which its licence needs; a household’s own photo shows bare in its frame. That reads “always with the credit” as “a photo with a credit always shows it”: see Proposed rule change.

Not the uploader’s name. Crediting “Photo by Milo Bedini” would put a person’s name on the recipe once it’s published (TAS-119), which UK GDPR asks us not to do without a reason.

The crop is the centre of the photo, as now (object-cover). Choosing the crop is out of this issue.

Can’t load (deleted, or the network fails): the filler for its ID shows, as for a Commons photo today.
