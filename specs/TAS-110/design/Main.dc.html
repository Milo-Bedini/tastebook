One squiggle, only where there’s good news. The offer squiggles “Join” and the signed-out page “invited”; docs/design.md allows the squiggle under one word and one italic word in Display XL, and a household’s name can be many words, so it never goes under the name. Already-a-member and the three dead links are plain titles, like the not-found page.

The offer is the household page’s Members list, read-only: Avatar and name in Body, 56 px rows with a Line hairline, in the API’s order (by name, since you’re not in it), with no “(you)” and no Remove. “Join household” comes after the list, so you see who you’d join before the button: it’s the screen’s one peach button, with the press shadow.

Signed out, the page only says a link works. No name, no members, no count (UK GDPR: anyone holding a forwarded link would see them). “Sign in to join” is the peach button; the bar’s own Sign in stays as on every signed-out page and goes to the same place.

Each dead link says which and what to do, with “Back to Tastebook” as its one button, as the not-found page. Signed in or out they look the same apart from the bar’s end; being a member wins, so Ann opening her own expired link sees “You’re already in The Bedinis”.
