The dialog says exactly what happens to the household you leave: who stays, by name, joined as a list with “stays” for one and “stay” for more, or that it will be deleted when you’re its only member. Its title names where you’re going. The confirm is the danger “Join household”, the same words as the button that opened it (Button never wraps, so not the household’s name); the way out stays “Cancel”, with focus on it when it opens.

Joined, you’re somewhere new: the page replaces the link in history and lands on /household, which shows The Bedinis with you first, Remove beside the others, and Invite someone and Leave below (off the bottom of this frame). The toast says where you went; focus starts at the top of the page, as for any move between pages.

A link that dies on the way (cancelled, expired, or its household gone) moves nobody: an info toast says why and the page redraws as that dead link, with focus on its title (tabindex −1, the in-text ring), so a screen reader hears what changed.

A failure is an alert toast worded like Leave’s, and nothing changes: “Join household” keeps focus, so pressing it again is the retry, and the dialog asks again.
