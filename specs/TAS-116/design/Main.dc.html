A row reads as the recipe will. The ingredient’s name, capitalised, in Label L, and under it the line’s detail in Body Muted: the quantity as the page writes it (“2–3 tbsp”, “0.5”), then the note, then “· optional”; nothing set reads “To taste”. Each row is one real button (its accessible name is “Olive oil 2–3 tbsp, extra virgin · optional”), at least 64 px tall, with the checkbox row’s Surface hover, 14 px corners and focus ring, and a Muted chevron that says it opens. Hairlines in Line between rows; no numbers, which would read like amounts.

A group is a section under a 2 px Line rule, headed by an h3 in Caption type, as the recipe page heads it, which holds its own row button. Its lines sit on a 2 px Line rail, then its own picker, “Add an ingredient to For the sauce”, so where a new line goes is never in doubt. A blank name reads “Unnamed group”.

Editing happens in one Dino Sheet, titled with the ingredient and saying where the line sits. Amount and Unit share a row with Amount’s help under both; Note; the Optional switch says what it means; Remove (secondary, Trash) and Done (primary) at the foot. Fields change the draft as typed, so Done, Close, Escape, the scrim and a drag all keep the edit, and focus goes back to the row, which shows it at once. Removing a line closes the sheet, says “Removed olive oil.” and puts focus in that list’s picker; removing a group says its lines joined the list above and puts focus on “Add a group”.

Reorder sits at the heading’s end once there are two entries, and is drawn on Reorder, checking and adding, with the errors, the add option and the loading states.
