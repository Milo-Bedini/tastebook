# Add a private recipe

Linear: TAS-114

## Summary

Every recipe today is a catalogue recipe: public, loaded from `catalogue.json`, with every detail filled in. This lets a signed-in member type a recipe into a form and save it as a private recipe that only their household can see. Only the title is required; whatever is missing is named on the recipe's page. Signed in, search and the filters cover the household's private recipes as well as the catalogue, each private one marked. Loading the catalogue leaves household recipes alone. It comes now because editing (TAS-115), ingredients (TAS-116), the method (TAS-117), publishing (TAS-119), deleting (TAS-120) and copies (TAS-126) all build on a recipe a household owns.

The work ships in two PRs, each merging to `main` on its own:

- **Part 1 (this issue, TAS-114): the API.** The recipe's household and author, optional details, `POST /api/recipes/`, private recipes in the list and on their page for their household only, the catalogue load scoped to catalogue recipes, the outsiders test, and reading pages that cope with a missing detail.
- **Part 2 (a sub-issue filed at approval): the form and the marks.** The form page at `/recipes/new`, the "Add a recipe" button, the private mark on cards and the recipe page, and the notice naming what's missing, built to the approved design.

Evidence: feature. Part 2 adds a page, a button, a mark and a notice. Part 1 changes nothing a person sees on a catalogue recipe: private recipes exist only through the API until part 2.
Design: needed. The form is a new screen, and the private mark and the missing-parts notice are new looks; no board in `docs/design/` draws them. The design draws part 2; part 1 builds without it.
Components: Private mark (new), TimeBlobs (changed: one time, for a recipe with only a prep or a cook time). The form reuses Field, Input, TextArea, FilterChip, SegmentedControl, Switch, Button, Banner and Note; the design stage says so if it needs more.

## Product behaviour

One claim per acceptance criterion, in the issue's order, with the part that delivers it:

1. Signed in, you can add a recipe by typing its title, description, cuisines, courses, difficulty, times, servings or yield, one-pot and source credit into a form. (Part 2; part 1's API takes every one.)
2. Only the title is required; a private recipe's page names its missing ingredients, method, prep time, cook time and servings. (Part 2; part 1 saves a recipe with only a title.)
3. A new recipe is private: only its household's members can find or open it. (Part 1)
4. Signed in, search and filters cover your household's private recipes and public ones, each private one marked; signed out, public only. (Part 1 for what's found, part 2 for the mark.)
5. Loading the catalogue leaves household recipes, their cuisines and their courses alone. (Part 1)
6. The outsiders test probes adding a recipe and opening, listing and searching a private one. (Part 1)

What else holds, each proved by a test below:

- A time filter leaves out a recipe missing that time; a difficulty filter leaves out one with no difficulty.
- A private recipe's address is its title plus six random characters (`/recipes/grandmas-curry-k7m2qx`), so it never says another household has a recipe of that title.
- Signing out on a private recipe's page leaves it: the page says "Recipe not found".
- Signed out, "Add a recipe" leads to sign-in and back to the form. (Part 2)
- A catalogue recipe still needs its difficulty, prep and cook times, servings or a yield, and a source credit: a catalogue file missing one fails the load.

## Technical design

### How it works today

- `Recipe` (`backend/recipes/models.py:154`) has no owner. `difficulty`, `prep_minutes`, `cook_minutes` and `source` are required; `cuisines` and `courses` have no `blank=True`; `recipe_servings_or_yield` (`:226`) demands exactly one of servings and a yield. `total_minutes` (`:265`) adds all three times.
- `Recipe.save()` (`:258`) gives a recipe without a slug `unique_slug_for(title)`, which numbers a taken slug (`grandmas-curry-2`).
- `RecipeListView` and `RecipeDetailView` (`views.py:466`, `:491`) set `authentication_classes = []`, so they never see the session, and list every recipe.
- `load_catalogue` (`services.py:52`) deletes every recipe the file doesn't list (`:65`), matches the file's slugs against every recipe (`:141`), and `_link` (`:227`) deletes every cuisine, course and equipment link it didn't load, whichever recipe it belongs to.
- The recipe admin (`admin.py:102`) is read-only and says every row comes from `catalogue.json`.
- On the frontend, `useRecipes` and `useRecipe` (`recipes-api.ts`) are keyed `['recipes', …]` and don't depend on the session. Sign-in and sign-out forget only the household (`forgetHousehold`, `household-api.ts:13`).

### The flow

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
  participant Form as /recipes/new (part 2)
  participant API as POST /api/recipes/
  participant Service as recipes.services.add_recipe
  participant DB
  Form->>API: { title, …optional details } with the CSRF header
  API->>API: signed in with a household? else 403
  API->>API: NewRecipeSerializer: types, lengths, choices, known slugs; else 400
  API->>Service: add_recipe(account, details, cuisines, courses)
  Service->>DB: Recipe(household, created_by, private slug), full_clean(), save, set cuisines and courses
  Service-->>API: ValidationError (a pair half given)? 400
  API-->>Form: 201 Recipe (visibility "private")
  Form->>Form: go to /recipes/<slug>
```

### Part 1: the API

#### Model (`backend/recipes/models.py`, edit; migration `0014_household_recipes.py` from `makemigrations recipes`)

- `Recipe.household = models.ForeignKey('households.Household', null=True, blank=True, on_delete=models.CASCADE, related_name='recipes', help_text='Empty for a catalogue recipe.')`. A recipe with a household is a household recipe; one without is a catalogue recipe.
- `Recipe.created_by = models.ForeignKey(settings.AUTH_USER_MODEL, null=True, blank=True, on_delete=models.SET_NULL, related_name='recipes_written', help_text='Who wrote it. Empty for a catalogue recipe.')`. The API never sends it.
- `difficulty`: `blank=True` (empty is `''`, as the other text fields are).
- `prep_minutes`, `cook_minutes`: `null=True, blank=True`.
- `source`: `blank=True`.
- `cuisines`, `courses`: `blank=True`.
- `recipe_servings_or_yield` allows neither: add `| models.Q(servings__isnull=True, yield_amount__isnull=True, yield_unit='')`, message `'Give servings or a yield with its unit, not both.'`.
- New constraint, so a catalogue file can't leave a detail out:

```py
models.CheckConstraint(
    condition=models.Q(household__isnull=False)
    | ~models.Q(difficulty='')
    & models.Q(prep_minutes__isnull=False, cook_minutes__isnull=False)
    & ~models.Q(source='')
    & (models.Q(servings__isnull=False) | models.Q(yield_amount__isnull=False)),
    name='recipe_catalogue_is_complete',
    violation_error_message=(
        'A catalogue recipe needs a difficulty, prep and cook times, servings or a yield, and a source credit.'
    ),
),
```

- `class Visibility(models.TextChoices)`: `PUBLIC = 'public', 'Public'`, `PRIVATE = 'private', 'Private'`. Not stored: `Recipe.visibility` is a property, `Visibility.PUBLIC if self.household_id is None else Visibility.PRIVATE`, until TAS-119 makes a household recipe public.
- `total_minutes -> int | None`: `None` unless `prep_minutes` and `cook_minutes` are both given; otherwise the sum of all three, as today.
- `RecipeQuerySet` gains:

```py
def catalogue(self) -> RecipeQuerySet:
    return self.filter(household__isnull=True)

def visible_to(self, user: User | AnonymousUser) -> RecipeQuerySet:
    """Catalogue recipes, and the recipes of the user's household when they're signed in to one."""
    household_id = user.household_id if user.is_authenticated else None
    if household_id is None:
        return self.catalogue()
    return self.filter(Q(household__isnull=True) | Q(household_id=household_id))

def private_slug_for(self, title: str) -> str:
    """The title's slug and six random characters, so it says nothing about other households' titles."""
```

- `taking_at_most` and `with_difficulties` stay as they are: a `NULL` time makes the `lte` false and `''` is never a chosen difficulty, so both leave the recipe out (Decisions).
- `backend/recipes/slugs.py` (edit): `SUFFIX_ALPHABET = 'abcdefghjkmnpqrstuvwxyz23456789'` (no `i`, `l`, `o`, `0`, `1`) and `random_suffix() -> str`, six `secrets.choice` picks. `private_slug_for` returns `f'{base_slug(title)}-{random_suffix()}'`, drawing again while `self.filter(slug=…).exists()`.
- `docs/architecture.md:34` and `GLOSSARY.md` change with it (Docs).

#### Service (`backend/recipes/services.py`, edit)

```py
def add_recipe(author: User, details: dict[str, Any], cuisines: list[Cuisine], courses: list[Course]) -> Recipe:
    """Saves a private recipe in the author's household. Raises ValidationError when a pair is half given
    (servings or a yield, extra time and its label)."""
```

In `transaction.atomic()`: build `Recipe(household_id=author.household_id, created_by=author, slug=Recipe.objects.private_slug_for(details['title']), **details)`, call `full_clean()` (it runs the model's constraints), `save()`, then `cuisines.set(...)` and `courses.set(...)`. The caller checks the author has a household.

Scope the catalogue load to catalogue recipes:

- `:65`: `Recipe.objects.catalogue().exclude(pk__in=…).delete()`, and the comment becomes "Catalogue recipes the file no longer lists go; household recipes aren't the file's."
- `:141`: `existing = dict(Recipe.objects.catalogue().values_list('slug', 'pk'))`, so a file slug never takes over a household recipe.
- `_link`: read and delete only the catalogue's rows, `through.objects.filter(**{f'{field.m2m_field_name()}__household__isnull': True})`.
- `_read`: refuse the slug `new`, which `/recipes/new` would hide: `CatalogueError('The recipe slug "new" is the address of the form for adding a recipe; give the recipe another slug')`.
- `_replace` needs no change: it touches only the recipes it loaded.

- `RecipeSummarySerializer` gains `visibility = serializers.ChoiceField(choices=Visibility.choices, read_only=True)` and lists it after `slug`. `total_minutes` becomes `IntegerField(read_only=True, allow_null=True)`. `extra_kwargs` adds `'difficulty': {'required': True, 'allow_blank': True}` (lesson `blank-model-fields-are-optional-in-the-schema.md`).
- `RecipeSerializer.Meta.extra_kwargs` adds `'prep_minutes': {'required': True, 'allow_null': True}`, `'cook_minutes': {'required': True, 'allow_null': True}`, `'source': {'required': True, 'allow_blank': True}`.
- New `NewRecipeSerializer(serializers.ModelSerializer)`, the request body:

## Decisions

- Ship as two PRs, the API first, then the form and the marks after design, because one would be about 25 files and 1,500 lines. Asked: two PRs? → Two parts.
- Record the author now as `Recipe.created_by`, nullable, never sent by the API, because TAS-119, TAS-120 and TAS-121 need it and a recipe saved before it exists could never get one. Asked: record who wrote it? → Record it now.
- A time limit leaves out a recipe missing that time, and total time is null unless prep and cook are both given, so a filter shows only recipes known to fit. SQL's `NULL` comparison does it with no code. Asked: missing time under a time filter? → Left out.
- A private recipe's page names missing ingredients, method, prep time, cook time and servings, and nothing else, so the notice stays short. Asked: which missing parts? → Plus times and servings.
- "Add a recipe" shows for everyone and a signed-out visitor goes through sign-in and back, because it tells visitors what an account is for. Asked: where to start? → Button for everyone.
- A private recipe's slug is its title plus six random characters, because a numbered slug tells you another household has that title. Catalogue recipes keep their file's slug. Asked: how is the address made? → Title plus a random code.
- Visibility is worked out from `household` and not stored, because every household recipe is private until TAS-119; TAS-119 stores it behind the same API field. (agent)
- `household` cascades on delete, because `SET_NULL` would turn a household's recipes into catalogue recipes everyone can see, and `PROTECT` would make leaving fail. Only a last member leaving deletes a household, and the household page doesn't offer that. (agent)
- An empty difficulty is `''`, not `null`, as `source`, `extra_label` and `yield_unit` are, and ruff's DJ001 refuses `null` on a text field. (agent)
- A database constraint keeps catalogue recipes complete, so a catalogue file that leaves a detail out still fails the build with a message, now that the fields themselves are optional. (agent)
- The service runs the model's constraints with `full_clean()`, and the view turns a failure into a 400, so the servings-or-yield and extra-time rules live in one place. (agent)
- Adding a recipe signed out, or with no household yet, answers 403, not the household endpoints' 404, because nothing is missing from the address. (agent)
- Sign-in and sign-out reset every query outside `households`, because a private recipe must leave the page and the cache when the session ends (OWASP ASVS 5.0, 14.3.1). (agent)
- The recipe admin lists catalogue recipes only, because its notice says every row comes from `catalogue.json` and staff have no need to read households' recipes (UK GDPR, data minimisation). (agent)
- The catalogue refuses the slug `new`, because `/recipes/new` would hide that recipe. (agent)
- Until part 2, a recipe with only one of prep and cook time shows no time blobs, since `TimeBlobs` takes two or three and its one-blob look waits for the design. Only API-made private recipes can have one. (agent)

## Out of scope

- Editing (TAS-115), ingredient lines (TAS-116), the method and equipment (TAS-117), the photo (TAS-118), publishing (TAS-119), deleting (TAS-120), keeping your recipes when you leave (TAS-121), copies (TAS-126) and share links (TAS-128).
- Showing the author on a recipe.
- When TAS-116 and TAS-117 land: the catalogue load deletes an ingredient or equipment the file drops, which a household recipe may use. TAS-116 and TAS-117 decide what happens then.
- Resetting cached recipes when someone joins, leaves or is removed from a household (see Assumptions).
- The list's empty state copy ("Recipes show here once they're in the catalogue.").

- Joining, leaving or being removed from a household doesn't reset cached recipes; the next page that mounts refetches them, so the old household's private recipes show at most until that refetch. Confirm with: Milo, at approval.

- The design stage runs before part 1 builds, though part 1 needs no design; part 1 can start once the spec is approved if the router allows. Confirm with: Milo, at approval.
- A household deleted with recipes in it takes them with it; only a last member leaving does that, which the household page doesn't offer, and TAS-121 decides what leaving does with your recipes. Confirm with: Milo, at approval.
