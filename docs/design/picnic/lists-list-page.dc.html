Who sees it, under the title: a mark with the private mark’s shape (10 px corners, Small Ink, a 2 px transparent border) on Surface: User and “Just you”, or Users and the household’s name. It describes and is never a control. The index says it by its groups.

Rename (secondary) and Delete list (ghost, Trash2) sit under the count, words not an icon, so neither hides in a menu. Any member can rename or delete a household list. Favourites has neither.

Each card’s action is Remove, an X icon button at its meta’s end named “Remove Bibimbap from Weeknight dinners”, where Favourites has its heart, and an unavailable recipe’s too. It acts at once, as Favourites’ heart does: the card goes, focus moves to the next card’s Remove (or the heading when none is left), and a toast says “Removed from Sunday roasts” with Undo, which puts it back in its place. Any member can remove from a household list. Adding to a list is TAS-125.
