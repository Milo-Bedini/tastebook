“Saved in …”, Muted Body, names each list that holds it as a link to its page, Favourites included, in the sheet’s order. Up to three are named; more ends “and 2 more lists”. In no list, there’s no line. It follows every tick, and the heart follows Favourites’ box.

Why a sheet: design.md’s Sheet is “for light tasks (adding a recipe to the list…)”, and it holds any number of lists and any name. Above about six rows the lists scroll inside the sheet under its fixed header.

No action button: each tick acts at once, so Close, Escape, the scrim and a drag are the way out, as on the New list sheet. The page keeps its one peach button (none here).

Private recipes too: a household’s private recipe gets the same button and sheet, and can go in any of Milo’s or The Bedinis’ lists (drawn on “The sheet’s other states”).
