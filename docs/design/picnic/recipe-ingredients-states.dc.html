Save recipe

Reorder is a mode, from a secondary button at the heading’s end (with ArrowUpDown, shown from two entries). Pressed, the same button reads “Done reordering”, so focus stays on it. The pickers, “Add a group” and the row buttons go: each line shows only its name, with Move up and Move down icon buttons named for it; each group shows its name in its h3 with its own pair. The status line, visually hidden in the list, shows under the heading here, so the last move stays on screen. A line next to a group heading crosses it: down, to the start of that group (“Moved onion into For the sauce.”); up, to the end of the list above (“Moved onion above the groups.”). A group moves with its lines past its neighbour. Focus stays on the button pressed, in its new place.

Nothing is disabled at the ends. As the Disabled rule allows (“leave the control enabled and explain on press”), the first and last buttons stay live and say why on press, so the list carries no reason lines and focus never rests on a dead control. No rule change is needed.

Save’s first error, in the form’s order, opens that row’s sheet on its field: focus goes to the row, then the sheet opens on Amount (or Group name), so closing it lands back on the row. Rows in error carry their message under their detail in Danger, with its icon, tied by aria-describedby; the field in the sheet takes Danger’s message in place of its help. The messages are the spec’s: unreadable, zero, falling and a blank group name.

Adding and loading touch only the pickers. “Adding “chipotle paste”…” and “Loading ingredients…” are status lines in Label type beside a spinner; “Couldn’t load the ingredients” is the one ErrorWithRetry. Rows already in the draft stay editable throughout.
