In the mode the equipment, the text areas, Remove and “Add a step” are gone: each step shows its number and its first two lines, with Move up and Move down. The buttons are named by number, and the number follows the step, so the announcement says both.

Equipment is a chip group, as Cuisines and Courses are. A fieldset with the legend “Equipment” and the eleven tools from the shared list as untilted filter chips (off: Field outline; on: mint with a check). It loads and fails as the other chip groups do (drawn on Method: removing, pasting, checking and loading).

A step is a number and a text area. The number is the Method’s look, Instrument Serif italic in Pop at 32 px, and it is the text area’s label (“Step 2” to a screen reader; the word is visually hidden, the number shown). The text area is Dino’s, unchanged: it grows with the step, never scrolls inside. Hairlines in Line between steps. Remove (an icon button, “Remove step 2”) sits at the end of the number’s row once there are two steps.

Writing is direct. A step is one field, so it’s typed where it sits, with no sheet to open (NN/g 7). Enter makes a new line inside the step; “Add a step” appends a blank one and puts focus in it. Blank steps are left out when the recipe saves, so a spare empty box never needs removing.

Reorder sits at the heading’s end once there are two steps and works as Ingredients’ does: the same element turns into “Done reordering”, every move button stays enabled, and pressing one that can’t move says why (“Step 1 is already first.”).

Laptop and tablet keep this shape: the form is one 640 px column from tablets up, as on the ingredients boards, so the steps just get wider.
