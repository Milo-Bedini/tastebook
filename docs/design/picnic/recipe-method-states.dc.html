What a dashed box quotes is the section’s status line, read out by screen readers and hidden on screen.

Add a step

Says “Removed step 2.”

Removing the last step puts focus on “Add a step”. Nothing is asked first: the recipe isn’t saved until Save, so leaving the page or putting it back undoes it.
Pasted a method · a method with blank lines between its paragraphs pasted into an empty step: each paragraph becomes a step
Scrolled to the steps

Says “Pasted 4 steps.” Focus is at the end of the last.

Only a paste into an empty step splits, and only at blank lines; a paste into a step with text, or with no blank line, goes in as typed. Typing a blank line never splits.
Save found a problem · Save pressed with a step the API refuses: focus goes to that step, its error under it

The error replaces nothing (a step has no help) and says how to fix it. The limit here is a stand-in: the build sets the real one, and the message names it.

Editing a recipe keeps its chosen equipment while the list loads, and Save keeps it if the list never does.

Every change is said. Removing (“Removed step 2.”), pasting (“Pasted 4 steps.”), adding (focus moves into the new step, whose label says its number), moving (“Moved step 3 up. It’s now step 2.”) and a move that can’t happen (“Step 3 is already last.”) all go through the section’s one status line (NN/g 1).

Blank steps are left out on Save, so a box left empty never stops a save or needs removing; a recipe saved with no steps says “No method yet” on its page, as today.
