Rename list is the New list sheet with the name alone: who it’s for doesn’t change, and the help line says who sees it (“Everyone in The Bedinis sees this list.” on a household one). The same errors as New list: unique among the owner’s lists (yours, or the household’s), and never “Favourites”. Favourites can’t be renamed.

Delete list asks first with ConfirmDialog as it is: the danger button names the action, the way out is Cancel. A personal list’s dialog drops the first sentence: “Its 7 recipes stay in Tastebook and in any other list.” While it deletes, the trigger reads “Deleting…”.

After deleting the list’s page is gone, so Milo lands on Lists with focus on its heading and a toast. A failed rename or delete keeps the sheet or page and says “Couldn’t delete the list. Check your connection, then try again.” in a toast with Try again.

Someone else deletes it while Milo has it open: the page shows “This list has gone. Someone in The Bedinis deleted it.” with All lists (ErrorWithRetry’s place, no retry).
