No reason: nothing is sent; each radio’s ring turns Danger and the error sits under the list in Small Danger with its icon, saying how to fix it. The group is described by it and focus goes to the first reason. The error goes once a reason is chosen.

Didn’t send: the reason and note are kept, so nothing is typed twice, and why sits over Send report, announced. Pressing it again is the retry. Signed out by then: off to sign-in and back, as on the page.

One report each: once you’ve reported a recipe, Report recipe stays “Reported”, disabled (it stays focusable) with its reason under it. Staff restoring it doesn’t give you another (a question for you).
