Two headings, in Title: “Just you” and the household’s name. A row says nothing about who sees it: the heading it sits under does, and a screen reader hears it as the section’s name.

Favourites stays first under “Just you”; the other lists follow by name, in each group.

New list is the screen’s one peach button, at the end of the title row (it wraps under the title at large text). It opens the New list sheet, where Milo picks who it’s for.

A household with no lists still shows its heading, with one line in Small Muted under it: “Lists you make for The Bedinis show here, for everyone in it.” So the place is learnt before it’s used.

A household of one (no one else has joined) still has the group: its lists will be shared with whoever joins.

Each row links to its list’s page: a personal list at /lists/<name>, a household one at /lists/household/<name>, so Milo’s own Christmas and The Bedinis’ Christmas can both exist without their addresses clashing.
