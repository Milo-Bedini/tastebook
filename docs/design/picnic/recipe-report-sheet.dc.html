Send report

A sheet, not a dialog: design.md keeps a Dialog for what can’t be undone and gives Sheet the light tasks; a report is a short form. Close, Escape, the scrim and a drag are the way out, and nothing is sent until Send report.

Send report is the sheet’s one peach button. The reason is a native radio group (arrow keys move it), each named by its reason with its line as its description. The note is a Text area, at most 500 characters, and optional for every reason.

What it doesn’t say: nothing promises the recipe goes away, as a recipe staff have restored stays up. The sheet names the recipe and its author, so you know what you’re reporting.

Sending: Send report turns to “Sending…” with aria-busy and ignores presses; the rows and note stay as they are. What happens next is on “After a report”.
