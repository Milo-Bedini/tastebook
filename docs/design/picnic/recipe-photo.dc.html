A JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC photo, up to 10 MB. It shows at the top of the recipe, cropped to this shape.

Good at: what you see is what the recipe shows (the same 7:5 crop in the same Paper frame), and an empty field is a big, plain target for a thumb. Bad at: it takes a screen’s height on a phone, so Cuisines and Courses sit further down.

Where it goes. After Description, in the form’s first group: Title stays first because it’s the only required field and where focus starts, and the photo sits with the other things that describe the recipe.

One button, then the system’s picker. “Add a photo” opens the device’s own chooser (an input type="file" accept="image/*"), which on a phone offers the camera or the library. No crop tool: the photo is centred and cropped by the page, as Commons photos are now.

It uploads as soon as it’s chosen, so Save recipe stays quick; the recipe keeps it only once it’s saved. The frame isn’t tilted: tilt is for polaroids you look at, and this one has controls under it.

Remove photo is a ghost button with its icon, so Save recipe stays the one peach button. It takes the photo out of the form at once and offers Undo in a toast (PhotoField’s board).

Assumed (Linear wasn’t reachable from this run, so the triage brief wasn’t read): the file types and the 10 MB limit, and that the form has one photo.
