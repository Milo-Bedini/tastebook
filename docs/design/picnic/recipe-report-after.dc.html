Your report hid it: the sheet closes and Milo goes back where they came from (All recipes, or the list they opened it from), since the page has nothing left to show them. The toast says what happened; focus goes to the page’s heading.

Restored before: staff have already looked once, so the recipe stays up for everyone. The sheet closes, focus goes to Reported, and the toast says only that staff will look. Nobody learns the recipe was reported before.

Anyone else: search and lists drop it, and its address shows “Recipe not found”, exactly as for a deleted recipe, so a hidden recipe says nothing about why. That includes Sam’s household (a question for you).

Saved in a list: it keeps its place as the unavailable recipe, as today, meta and all (“Made private or deleted”: a question for you). Once staff restore it, it’s back as a card.
