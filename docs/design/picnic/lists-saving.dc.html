Pressed signed out, it opens sign-in with next back to this recipe, and the save happens on the way back, with its toast (drawn here at the foot: no dock until signed in, then the dock appears).

On a laptop the heart keeps its place at the end of the title’s row, in the right-hand column (design.md, Layout), and the toast sits at the foot, centred.

Why optimistic: the heart is a toggle with no room for a “Saving…” line, so it shows the new state at once, takes aria-busy and ignores presses until the save settles, then keeps it or turns back with the error toast.
