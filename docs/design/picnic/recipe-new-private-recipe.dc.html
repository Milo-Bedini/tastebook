The private mark sits right under the title, before the description, so it’s read straight after the recipe’s name and its place matches what you see. It’s the same mark as on the card.

What’s missing is a warning Banner, not a butter Note. docs/design.md already gives Banner’s warning tone to an import’s gaps, which is the same job: a recipe that needs a look. Butter means time, and a butter note right under the butter time blob would blur the two. The Banner lists the spec’s five lines in its order, as a real list, and shows only on a private recipe with something missing; with nothing missing it’s gone. It has no action until editing (TAS-115) gives it “Edit recipe”.

A part the recipe lacks leaves no trace beyond the Banner: no empty facts line (today’s code renders an empty paragraph when every fact is missing; part 2 leaves it out), no blobs with no times, no servings stepper or units with no ingredients, no Ingredients or Method heading.

One time, one blob (middle frame): TimeBlobs keeps each blob a third of the row wide, so a lone Prep blob looks like the first of three rather than a stretched pill. Its board has the details.
