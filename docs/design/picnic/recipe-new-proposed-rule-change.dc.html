Proposed rule change

The private mark is the first label in Dino that is neither a control nor a sticker, so the shape rules have no corner for it. These edits name it, and record the uses the boards rely on. Highlighted words are new.
Rules › Shape

Why: a pill says “press me”, and the list has no corner for something you only read. 10 px is the nearest existing token, so no new token.

Now

Pills for controls (one-line fields among them), 24 px for cards and text areas, 32 px for sheets, 10 px for checkboxes, square for polaroids.

Proposed

Pills for controls (one-line fields among them), 24 px for cards and text areas, 32 px for sheets, 10 px for checkboxes and marks, square for polaroids.

Space and shape › Corners

Why: the same change where the corners are listed in full.

Now

… 20 px for notes and banners; 10 px for checkboxes; 14 px for a checkbox row’s hover fill …

Proposed

… 20 px for notes and banners; 10 px for checkboxes and a mark (the private mark); 14 px for a checkbox row’s hover fill …

Components › a new row

Why: the spec asks for the mark in the Components table; this is the board’s rule in words.

Now

No row.

Proposed

Private mark: a Lucide lock and “Private” in Small Ink, on Surface with a 2 px transparent border and 10 px corners. On a private recipe’s card, above its meta, and under its title on its page; a public recipe has none. It describes and is never a control: no hover, no tilt, never a pastel.

Components › Banner

Why: a private recipe’s missing parts are the same job as an import’s gaps, so they share the warning Banner rather than a butter Note, which means time.

Now

… warning for an import’s gaps, success for a clean import, danger for a failure …

Proposed

… warning for an import’s gaps or what a private recipe is still missing, success for a clean import, danger for a failure …

The fun, rationed › Blobs

Why: the spec asks the doc to name the one-blob TimeBlobs.

Now

Blobs (three fixed irregular shapes in peach, butter and mint) hold a recipe’s prep, cook and soaking times, and nothing else.

Proposed

Blobs (three fixed irregular shapes in peach, butter and mint) hold a recipe’s prep, cook and soaking times, and nothing else. A recipe shows only the times it has, one to three, in that order; each keeps its third of the row, and the colours and shapes go by position.
