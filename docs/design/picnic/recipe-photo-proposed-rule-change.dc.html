TAS-118 · docs/design.md, Space and shape, Photos

Proposed rule change

Now

Photos: in a polaroid (below), 4:3 in lists and 7:5 at the top of a recipe, always with the credit.

Proposed

Photos: in a polaroid (below), 4:3 in lists and 7:5 at the top of a recipe. always with the credit. A photo from Wikimedia Commons always has its credit; a photo a household uploads has none.

Why: the credit is there because Commons licences ask for the author, the licence and a link. A household’s own photo has no licence to honour, and the only credit we could give is the uploader’s name, which would go public with the recipe once it’s published (TAS-119): personal data the feature doesn’t need (UK GDPR, Apple Responsibility). The boards read today’s rule as “a photo with a credit always shows it”, which a Commons photo still does; this change writes that down, so review doesn’t ask for a credit an uploaded photo can’t give.

Also in the same paragraph, unchanged: the filler when there’s no photo, and the centred crop.
