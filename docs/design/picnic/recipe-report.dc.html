A ghost button, set apart: Lucide Flag and “Report recipe”, under a Line hairline after Save to list and Make a copy. It names what it does, keeps the page’s buttons about the recipe, and stays out of the way of cooking. No peach: the page still has no main action competing with the recipe.

Who sees it: anyone on a household’s public recipe who isn’t in its household, signed in or not. Catalogue recipes, private recipes and a shared-by-link page have none. It takes aria-haspopup="dialog", as it opens the sheet.

Signed out it still shows, so nobody has to know to sign in first: pressing it goes to sign-in and back, the way Make a copy does, and the sheet opens on return.
