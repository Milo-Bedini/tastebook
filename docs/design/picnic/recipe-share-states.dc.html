Turning on acts at once: it can be undone, and the switch is the undo (NN/g 3). While it saves, the switch is disabled with “Saving…”, as the Switch rule says, and moves only when the link exists; then the field appears under it and the toast isn’t needed, as the link itself confirms it.

Turning off asks first, in ConfirmDialog with the way out “Keep link”: it can’t be undone, because the link it kills won’t come back (turning on again makes a new one) and it reaches whoever holds it (Dialog rule). Turn off link is the danger button; focus starts on Keep link.

Copying is the household page’s invite link exactly: Copy link keeps focus and the toast says “Link copied”; refused, the toast says to select it, and focusing the field selects the whole address.

Made public: the share switch and its field go, and the old link shows “Recipe not found” (decided at Design Review). The page still has one switch, Public. Made private again, the share switch comes back off.

Only the author has either switch. Another member sees the private recipe as today; another household and someone signed out can’t open its page at all, only the link.

Failures (turning on, turning off) are toasts that name what didn’t happen and change nothing. Signed out meanwhile goes to sign-in and back, as the Public switch does.
