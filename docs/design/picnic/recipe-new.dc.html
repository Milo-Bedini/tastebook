One column at every width, in the spec’s order. The title, description, cuisines, courses, difficulty and one-pot sit first with no heading; then three Title section headings, “Times”, “Serves or makes” and “Where it’s from”, so a long form scans and a screen reader can jump between its parts. One-pot moved up beside difficulty, since it describes the dish rather than its timing; it’s still the spec’s Switch.

Short numbers get short fields. Times and amounts are 96 px pills with “minutes” after them in Muted (tied to the field with aria-describedby), so the field’s width says “a number goes here” (NN/g 5). Prep and cook share a row; extra time and what it’s for stack, with the spec’s help under “What it’s for”.

Serves or makes is the spec’s segmented control. Serves shows “How many people”; Makes shows “How many” and “What it makes”, with help “Cookies, flatbreads or jars.” Switching keeps what was typed in each, and only the chosen one is sent.

Difficulty has a fourth option, “None”, chosen as the form opens, so “no difficulty” is a choice you can see and go back to. The spec’s “Not set” wraps onto two lines in the segmented control at 390 px; “None” fits. The word above the control is decorative; the control’s own legend names it for screen readers, as on the account page.

The chip groups wait on their own. While they load, each group shows a spinner and “Loading cuisines…” in place of the chips (not Loading’s blank polaroids, which mean a list of recipes). A failure is ErrorWithRetry inside that group alone; Title and everything else keep working.
