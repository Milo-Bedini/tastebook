What the consumer provides

  • page and pageCount, from 1; with one page or none it renders nothing.
  • to(page): each link’s address, so the caller keeps its search and filters (?q=orzo&page=3; page 1 has no page).
  • label: the nav’s name, “Pages” by default.
  • Moving focus and scrolling after a page changes is the page’s job, not the component’s: it only links.

How it behaves

  • A nav holding, in order, Previous, “Page X of Y”, Next. Links named in words with a chevron (Lucide, 2 px stroke, hidden from screen readers).
  • No Previous on page 1, no Next on the last: hidden, not disabled. The other link keeps its place.
  • One row from 420 px of room (a container query); under it, the line above and the links half and half.
  • The range (“25–48 of 137 recipes”) is the list’s count line, above the list, not part of Pagination: the pager says the page, the count says the recipes.
  • Grows into page numbers (Variant B) if a list ever needs jumping; until then it stays this small.

Proposed docs/design.md row (Components)

Pagination · Moves through a long list a page at a time (design/dino/pagination.dc.html), under the list in a nav named “Pages”: Previous and Next are secondary buttons as links, named in words with their chevrons, and “Page 2 of 6” in Label between them. Page 1 has no Previous and the last page no Next; one page has no pager. One row from 420 px of room; narrower, the line sits above the two links, which share the row. The page is in the address.
