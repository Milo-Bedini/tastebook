Props: the same tabs as the Dock. It is named from 1024 px and compact below by a media query, so the page never chooses; compact icon-only tabs take their label as their name.

In the TopBar: TopBar takes the tabs as a prop and places them after the wordmark, so the bar’s order is wordmark, tabs, search, account. It draws neither tabs nor dock with fewer than two.

Contrast: On pastel on Peach 9.5, Ink on the ground 16.4, Ink on Surface 14.2; the focus ring in Ink is 16.4 against the ground.
