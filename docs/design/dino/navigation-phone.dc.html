Every page with the dock keeps 104 px free at its foot (the dock’s 62 px, the 20 px under it and 22 px of air), so the last card, button or step scrolls clear of it. The dock stays put while the page scrolls under it.

It’s the same nav as the tabs: links in a list named “Main”, after the top bar in the page’s order, so focus meets it before the page, at every width. The dock only places it at the foot.

Signed out there’s only Recipes, and with one tab there’s no navigation at all (docs/design.md, Layout): the page runs to the foot.
