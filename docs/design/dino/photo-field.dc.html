Props it should grow into: label, help, error (as Field), photo (a Photo, a chosen file being uploaded with its progress, or none), shape (hero 7:5 here; card 4:3 if another form ever needs one), onChoose(file), onRemove(), onRetry(), onCancel(). The upload itself lives in the recipes area’s API hook, not in Dino.

Checked on choosing: the type and size are refused before any request, with the file’s own type and size in the words (NN/g 5 and 9). Tastebook checks them again on upload; its refusal shows as Couldn’t upload, with its message in place of the connection line. A photo too big to send (this browser can’t make it smaller, or Tastebook answers 413) can’t change on a retry, so it shows as Too big to send, with no Try again.

Screen readers: the field is a group named “Photo” and described by its help or error. “Uploading the photo” and “Photo uploaded” are announced in a polite status; the percentage is visual only, so it doesn’t chatter. The preview’s alt text is “The photo for <title>”, falling back to “The photo for this recipe”.

Not here: no drag handle, crop, rotate or filters. HEIC can’t show in most browsers, so its preview waits for Tastebook’s first thumbnail; until then the faded box shows the place-setting filler.
