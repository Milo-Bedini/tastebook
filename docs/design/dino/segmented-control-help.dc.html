  • What changes: two optional props. labelVisible shows the legend above the track in Label (Ink), instead of only to screen readers, as a form field’s label is. description puts a line in Small Muted 6 px under the track, joined to the fieldset by aria-describedby, so it’s read on entering the group.
  • Unchanged: the track, the mint chosen segment with its check, hover, pressed, the one tab stop and arrow keys, forced colours. Existing uses pass neither prop and look as they do now.
  • Why not new ChoiceCards: two options with one line of help need no new component; the line says what the chosen option means, so nobody has to remember what “Household” implies.
  • The line follows the choice, with no live region: the newly chosen segment’s name is announced as it is, and the line is there on the next read.
  • Copy: “Only you see it.” and “Everyone in <household> sees it and can change it.”
