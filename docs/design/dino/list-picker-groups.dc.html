Props, a suggestion: ListPicker is one group, a fieldset with its legend; its children are ListPickerRows, each taking name, count (the caller’s words: “7 recipes”, “Nothing here yet”), checked, onCheckedChange, saving and error. The caller stacks the groups 16 px apart and puts them in order.

What a screen reader hears: each row is a native checkbox named by the list and described by its count, so “Sunday roasts, 12 recipes, checkbox, checked”, inside its group “The Bedinis”. Saving adds aria-busy; didn’t save describes the box by the line under it too.

Every row is 56 px or taller (the story’s 44 px check measures the row, which is the target), the box shrinking to 92% only while pressed. Nothing here is coloured alone: on is a check, an error has its icon and words.
