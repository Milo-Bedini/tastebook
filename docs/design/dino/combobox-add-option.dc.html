How it works

  • addOption?: { label, onAdd }: the caller passes it only when it should show, with its label (“Add “chipotle paste””) and what it does.
  • It’s the list’s last item, after the options: an li role="option" with id …-add. Arrow keys, Enter, aria-activedescendant and the active fill treat it as one more option; ArrowUp from closed lands on it.
  • Its look is an option’s (Body Ink, 48 px, 16 px corners; active: a Line fill, the focus outline on its own edge and the Enter icon) with a Lucide Plus before the label. The typed text is bold, as matched letters are.
  • Below matches, a 1 px Line hairline 14 px in from each side, 6 px above it, sets it apart; alone, it has none.
  • Picking it calls onAdd() and closes the list; the search stays for the caller, who clears it once the line is added.

What it may grow into

  • Another caller’s add: “Add “Thursday” as a new list” in planning, or a new cuisine, with the same prop and no new look.
  • A busy state inside the option (a spinner in place of the Plus while onAdd runs), if a second caller needs one; for now the recipe form says “Adding…” under the picker.
  • Under forced colours the active option’s outline and the hairline draw in CanvasText, as the options’ do; the Plus is currentColor.
