Props: legend, name, options (each a value, label and optional hint), value, onValueChange, and an optional error. The legend is Label, as List picker’s; each hint describes its radio.

Behaviour is the platform’s: native radios in a fieldset, one tab stop, arrow keys move the choice; the whole row is the target. The box is round (a choice of one), where List picker’s and Checkbox row’s are square (any number).

Grows into any short single choice that needs explaining: a list’s privacy, a household’s role. Two to four options with short names stay a Segmented control; long lists of plain names stay a native select.
